How would you design a hotel management system? Let's break down the object-oriented design of a hotel management system, step-by-step. System requirements. The system should support booking of different room types like standard, deluxe, family suite, and business suite. 

Guests should be able to search rooms and make bookings. Receptionists can create bookings, check-in, and check out customers. The system should maintain housekeeping logs for rooms. 

Customers should be able to pay bills through credit card, check, or cash. The system should send notifications for booking, cancellation, and check-in reminders. Five main actors.

Guest, who searches available rooms and makes a booking. Receptionist, who adds and modifies rooms, creates bookings, checks-in, and checks out customers. Manager, responsible for adding new workers. 

Housekeeper, who adds or modifies housekeeping records. And the system, responsible for sending notifications for room booking and cancellation. Key classes. 

Hotel is the main class, which has a name, address, and list of rooms. Room has a room number, room style, room status, booking price, and is assigned a room key. Room booking manages bookings for a room, tracking check-in and check-out dates, and booking status. 

Room house. Keeping tracks. All housekeeping records. 

Room charge encapsulates details about room services that guests have requested. Invoice contains different invoice items for every charge against the room. Notification handles sending alerts to guests. 

Account, person, guest, receptionist, and server represent the different people interacting with the system. Key activity diagrams. Room booking. 

Guest or receptionist searches for available rooms, selects a room, confirms booking, and the system sends a notification. Check-in. Guest arrives. 

Receptionist verifies booking, assigns room key, updates room status to occupied. Cancel booking. Guest or receptionist cancels. 

System updates status and sends cancellation notification. That's the OOD of a hotel management system. Follow for more system design breakdowns.
